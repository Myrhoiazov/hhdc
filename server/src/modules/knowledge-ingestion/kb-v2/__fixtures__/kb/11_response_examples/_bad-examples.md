# Bad Examples

## Too little information

"Здравствуйте. Адрес \[ADDRESS\]."

Why bad: - ignores relevant age/group; - no next step; - feels cold.

## Too much irrelevant information

Customer asks for Rotterdam location, but reply lists every DDC city and
dance style.

Why bad: - increases cognitive load; - does not focus on intent.

## Hallucinated availability

"Да, место в группе точно есть."

Why bad: Published schedule does not prove availability.

## Example used as fact

Copying an old example's Monday 17:00 schedule without retrieving
current schedule.

Why bad: Examples define style, not current facts.
