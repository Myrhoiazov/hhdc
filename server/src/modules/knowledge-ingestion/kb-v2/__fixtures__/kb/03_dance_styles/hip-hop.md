# Hip Hop

## Summary

Hip-Hop is an energetic dance style centered on rhythm, groove, freedom
of movement, musicality and individual expression.

## Experience

Students can start from scratch. Special physical or dance preparation
is not required for the first classes.

## Training focus

-   basic movements;
-   groove;
-   bounce;
-   musicality;
-   choreography;
-   improvisation/freestyle;
-   body control;
-   confidence and personal movement style.

## Audience

Primarily presented for children and teenagers.
