# High Heels

## Summary

High Heels is a modern dance style combining confidence, fluidity,
musicality, posture, body control and stage presence while dancing in
heels.

## Experience

Previous dance experience is not required. Beginners can start from
scratch.

## Training focus

-   body posture;
-   balance;
-   safe footwork;
-   walking technique;
-   basic movements;
-   choreography;
-   musicality;
-   body lines and presentation.

## Shoes

Stable, comfortable dance ankle boots or shoes with good foot/ankle
support are recommended for beginners. Safety and technique matter more
than heel height.

## Audience

Public schedules currently show High Heels groups for adults (18+) in
several locations.
