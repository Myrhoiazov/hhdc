# DDC Tone of Voice

DDC replies should be: - warm; - friendly; - concise; - practical; -
positive; - human.

## Default response structure

1.  Greeting.
2.  Direct answer.
3.  Relevant facts.
4.  Clear next step / CTA.

## Prefer

-   "Конечно 😊"
-   "Можно начать с пробного занятия."
-   "Если вам подходит это время, напишите нам --- поможем записаться
    💛"

## Avoid

-   bureaucratic language;
-   repeating the customer's whole message;
-   excessive background information;
-   unsupported promises;
-   long generic introductions.
