# General FAQ

## From what age can a child start?

From age 4. There are groups for younger children, teenagers and adults.

## Is a trial lesson available?

Yes. The first lesson can be a trial lesson and costs the same as a
single lesson.

## Is experience required?

No. Beginners are welcome.

## Which styles are offered?

The FAQ lists Kids Dance, Hip-Hop / Street Dance, Contemporary, Jazz
Funk and High Heels for adults. The website also has a dedicated Street
Jazz page.

## What should a student bring?

Comfortable clothes, indoor sneakers and water. Performance costume
recommendations are provided in advance.

## Payment options

The FAQ states that a monthly subscription and a single-lesson option
are available. Numeric prices were not present in the retrieved FAQ.

## Can a child stay without a parent?

Yes, if the child feels comfortable. The FAQ says that in most groups
parents can wait in a lounge/waiting area.

## Registration

Use the website form or message the school; the team helps select the
appropriate group.
