# FAQ --- Trial Lessons

## Can I try before subscribing?

Yes. A trial lesson is available.

## What is the price?

Use current pricing data. If no numeric price is available in
authoritative knowledge, do not invent it.

## What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, indoor sneakers and water. For High Heels, use
suitable stable footwear according to the style guidance.

## What happens after the trial?

If the student likes the class, staff can help with the next
registration/subscription step.
