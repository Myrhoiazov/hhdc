# FAQ --- Children

## From what age can children start?

DDC publicly states that children can start from age 4. Exact groups
depend on the current city schedule.

## Does my child need experience?

No. Beginner groups/classes are available and children can start from
zero.

## How do I choose a group?

Use age + city first, then style preference. If unclear, recommend a
trial lesson.

## Can my child stay without a parent?

The public FAQ says yes if the child feels comfortable; in many cases
parents can wait in a waiting/lounge area. Do not promise that parents
can stay inside the class.
