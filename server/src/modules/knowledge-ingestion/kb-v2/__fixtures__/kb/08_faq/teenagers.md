# FAQ --- Teenagers

## Can I start as a teenager with no experience?

Yes. DDC welcomes beginners. The suitable group depends on age, city,
style and current schedule.

## I am 12/13/14/15. Which group should I choose?

Use the current schedule and match the student's exact age and city. If
several groups fit, ask about preferred style or suggest a trial lesson.

## What teenage classes are available in Rotterdam?

The current factual schedule should be retrieved. Do not answer from
this FAQ alone because schedules can change.

## I am 11 but want a 12+ group. Can I join?

Do not automatically confirm an age exception. Ask for exact age and, if
useful, experience, then route to staff confirmation.

## I don't know which style to choose.

Briefly explain the relevant styles available in that city and suggest a
trial lesson.
