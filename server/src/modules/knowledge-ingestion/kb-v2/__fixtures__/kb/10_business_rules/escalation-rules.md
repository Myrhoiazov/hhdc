# Escalation Rules

## Usually safe to answer automatically

-   locations;
-   published schedules;
-   dance styles;
-   age groups;
-   what to bring;
-   beginner questions;
-   general trial-lesson information;
-   general registration flow.

## Staff confirmation required

-   availability when CRM does not confirm it;
-   special discounts;
-   refunds;
-   payment disputes;
-   age exceptions;
-   subscription changes/cancellation when exact rules are unavailable;
-   complaints about a teacher;
-   injuries/medical accommodation;
-   private lessons/custom schedules;
-   exceptional camp arrangements.
