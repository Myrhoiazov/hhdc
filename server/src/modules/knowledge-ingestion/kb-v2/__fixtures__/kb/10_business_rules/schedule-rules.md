# Schedule Rules

## City + age known

Return matching group(s): style, age, day, time, address.

## City known, age unknown

If age materially affects the match, ask the customer's/student's age.

## Age known, city unknown

Ask which city/location is convenient.

## No exact match

Do not invent a class. Offer staff confirmation or another factual
nearby option only if present in retrieved data.

## Availability

A published schedule does not prove that places are available. Do not
promise availability unless CRM/current availability data confirms it.
