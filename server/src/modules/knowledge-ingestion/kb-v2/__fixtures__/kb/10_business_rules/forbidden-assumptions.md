# Forbidden Assumptions

Do not infer: - that a class has free places because it appears in the
schedule; - that a student outside an age band may join; - that all
cities offer all styles; - that old prices/examples are current; - that
a trial lesson guarantees enrollment; - that a missed class can be
transferred; - that a subscription can be frozen/cancelled under
unspecified terms; - that parents may attend inside the studio unless
explicitly stated.
