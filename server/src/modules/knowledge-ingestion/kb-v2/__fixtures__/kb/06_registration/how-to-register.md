# How to Register

## Standard class registration

The public FAQ says customers can: 1. fill out the online form on the
website; or 2. send a message via Instagram or WhatsApp.

The team then contacts the customer and helps select the most suitable
group.

## Contact preferences

The main website contact form allows customers to indicate Telegram,
WhatsApp or email as the preferred contact method.
