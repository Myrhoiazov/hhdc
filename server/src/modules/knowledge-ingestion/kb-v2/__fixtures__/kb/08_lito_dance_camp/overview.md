# LITO DANCE CAMP 2027

## Summary

An organized dance summer camp in Santa Susanna, Spain.

## Dates

26 July -- 4 August 2027

## Duration

10 days

## Accommodation

Hotel Don Angel 4★, Santa Susanna, Spain.

## Program

-   2--3 dance classes per day;
-   beach/sea time;
-   activities;
-   evening parties, games and discos;
-   organized group experience;
-   digital-detox concept with no regular gadget use.

## Positioning

The camp emphasizes dance development, friendship, independence,
confidence and real-world experiences away from constant
phone/social-media use.
