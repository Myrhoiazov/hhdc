# LITO DANCE CAMP --- Parents & Safety

## Supervision

Children are supervised by the camp team, choreographers and
accompanying adults.

## Insurance

Medical insurance is included.

## Accommodation and meals

Organized group accommodation in a 4★ hotel with three meals daily.

## Parent contact

The team agrees with parents in advance on a communication channel for
updates and contact with children.

## Illness

If a child becomes ill, the team contacts the parents and helps arrange
medical care locally.

## Age eligibility

Participant age is confirmed individually during booking. Parents should
provide the child's age and the camp team confirms suitability.

## Travel without parents

The camp is designed as an organized trip for children and teenagers.
Chaperoning and document details are discussed with parents before
booking.
