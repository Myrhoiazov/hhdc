# Age Groups

## General

Talent Center DDC works with: - children from age 4; - teenagers; -
adults.

Groups are organized by age and skill level.

## Examples from the current public schedule

-   Kids: 5--7
-   Kids: 7--11
-   Teen/older youth: 12+
-   Contemporary: 16+
-   Adult groups: 18+

Exact age requirements depend on the city, style and current schedule.
