# Trial Lesson

## Availability

A trial lesson is available.

## Price rule

The FAQ states that the first/trial lesson costs the same as a single
lesson.

## Purpose

A trial lesson lets a student: - meet the instructor; - experience the
atmosphere; - try the class; - choose a suitable dance style/group.

## Registration

Customers can use the online form or contact the school through
Instagram/WhatsApp; the team then helps find a suitable group.

## Important

The public FAQ does not provide a numeric single-lesson price in the
retrieved content. Do not invent one.
