# What to Bring to Class

The FAQ recommends: - comfortable clothing; - indoor sneakers; -
water; - a positive attitude.

For performances, costume recommendations are provided in advance.

For High Heels, appropriate stable shoes with good support are
recommended; beginners should prioritize safety and comfort.
